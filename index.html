The dishes you cannot leave without trying, the cultures behind them, when lobster and conch are in season, and how to order like a local. Come hungry.

Build a Belizean meal What’s in season
Quick answer

Start with rice and beans with stew chicken, fry jacks for breakfast, hudut on the Garifuna coast, salbutes and garnaches as snacks, and conch ceviche or grilled lobster when they are in season (lobster July–February; conch from October until the quota closes it). Add Marie Sharp’s hot sauce and a cold Belikin.

Belize is a small country with an outsized table. In a single day you can eat Mestizo salbutes cooked over an open hearth in San Pedro, slurp shrimp ceviche on a Caye Caulker dock, and finish with a Garifuna coconut fish stew that has simmered since morning in a Hopkins kitchen. The food is the product of six cultures that learned to cook side by side, Creole, Garifuna, Mestizo, Maya, Chinese and East Indian, each bringing its own staples and all of them borrowing from the others.

This guide is about what to eat and why. For named restaurants in San Pedro, Caye Caulker, Placencia, Hopkins and San Ignacio, see our sister site Best Restaurants Belize.

On Ambergris Caye

San Pedro’s walkable food scene

San Pedro packs the densest dining in the country into a handful of walkable blocks. Front Street runs the waterfront splurges, the middle streets hold reliable mid-range plates, and the back streets hide the local kitchens where Belizeans actually eat.

Spend an evening drifting from one to the next, ceviche and a cold Belikin at a beachfront table, then a plate of stew chicken and rice and beans a block inland, and you will understand why so many trips here are planned around the next meal. Named picks are on our San Pedro restaurant page.

Eat like a local

The street-food cart is where it’s at

Some of the best-value food in Belize never comes with a menu. Roadside carts and market stalls turn out salbutes, garnaches, panades and small corn tacos for a few dollars: hot, fresh and gone in a couple of bites. The tacos in our photo are a Mexican-style version; Belizean street tacos are usually small corn tortillas with shredded meat.

Follow the crowd at lunchtime, order what they are ordering and keep small bills handy. Read the street food guide.

Belize food FAQ

What is the national dish of Belize?

Rice and beans with stew chicken is widely considered Belize’s national plate. The rice and red beans are cooked together in coconut milk, and the chicken is slow-stewed in a red sauce built on recado (annatto). It usually comes with potato salad or coleslaw and fried plantain.

What food is Belize famous for?

Rice and beans, stew chicken and fry jacks from the Creole kitchen; hudut from the Garifuna coast; salbutes, garnaches, panades and escabeche from the Mestizo north; tamales and chocolate with Maya roots; and reef seafood such as lobster, conch ceviche and whole fried snapper in season.

When is lobster season in Belize?

From July 1 to the end of February. The closed season runs from March 1 to June 30 under Statutory Instrument 128 of 2021. Older guides that say June 15 to February 14 are out of date.

When is conch season in Belize?

Conch opens on October 1 and closes on June 30, or earlier if the national quota is reached. In 2026 the season closed more than two months early for that reason. It is closed from July 1 to September 30.

What is hudut?

The signature Garifuna dish: fish simmered in a coconut-milk broth called sere, served with fu-fu, plantain boiled and mashed smooth. Hopkins and Dangriga are the places to eat it.

What are fry jacks?

Puffed, golden pieces of fried dough eaten at breakfast, plain with honey or jam, or filled with beans, eggs and cheese.

Is Belizean food spicy?

Not usually. Dishes are seasoned with recado, onion, garlic and coconut rather than chili heat, and you add habanero sauce at the table to taste.

What should vegetarians eat in Belize?

Beans and rice (ask whether lard or meat stock was used), garnaches, fry jacks with beans and cheese, cassava bread, plantain and abundant fruit. Our meal builder can filter for dishes that are typically vegetarian.

Is Belizean food gluten-free friendly?

Much of it is: corn tortillas, salbutes, garnaches, panades, rice and beans, grilled fish and cassava dishes are usually wheat-free. Fry jacks, johnny cakes and meat pies are not. Confirm frying oil if you are sensitive.

What is the difference between "rice and beans" and "beans and rice"?

"Rice and beans" are cooked together, often in coconut milk. "Beans and rice" are cooked separately, with the beans served as a stew on the side.

What should I drink in Belize?

Belikin beer, local rum and rum punch, fresh juices and, if you are curious, a seaweed shake. Marie Sharp’s habanero sauce is the table condiment. See the drinks page.
