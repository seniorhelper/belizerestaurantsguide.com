How the builder works

The builder draws on the same 31 dishes as the decoder above, published in our open dish data file. Each dish has a culture, the times of day it is usually eaten, and whether it typically works as a starter or snack, a main, or something to drink or finish with. Pick a time of day and, if you like, cultures and diet preferences; the builder chooses one dish for each part of the meal at random from those that fit, so pressing again gives you a new combination.

"Classic" sticks to dishes most visitors enjoy straight away. "Adventurous" leans toward gibnut, cow foot soup, relleno, darasa and seaweed shakes. Seafood dishes carry their legal season, because lobster and conch are protected for part of the year. Vegetarian and gluten-free flags mean "typically": recipes vary by cook, so always ask.

The bucket list replaces our old "taste-of-Belize meter". It is a simple checklist that remembers your ticks on this device; nothing is uploaded. When you are ready to eat, our sister site lists named restaurants by town at Best Restaurants Belize.
