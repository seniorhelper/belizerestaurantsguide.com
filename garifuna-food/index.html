Food culture

Garifuna food: coconut, plantain and cassava

Patient, coconut-rich cooking from the people who settled Belize’s southern coast in the early 1800s.

The Garifuna descend from West African and Indigenous Carib and Arawak peoples of St. Vincent. Exiled by the British in the late 1790s, they settled along the Caribbean coast of Central America, reaching southern Belize in the early 19th century. Their arrival is celebrated each year on Garifuna Settlement Day, November 19, with drumming, dancing and food, especially in Dangriga and Hopkins. Garifuna language, dance and music are recognized by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage.

The three pillars

Garifuna cooking rests on coconut, plantain and green banana, and cassava, combined with fish from the sea. Coconuts are grated and squeezed by hand for their milk; plantains are boiled and pounded; cassava is grated, pressed and dried.

Hudut and sere

The signature meal pairs sere, fish simmered in coconut milk with herbs, with fu-fu, plantain boiled and mashed smooth in a wooden mortar. Together they are called hudut. You tear off a piece of fu-fu, dip it in the broth and eat it with the fish. Because it is slow to prepare, many kitchens ask you to order ahead.

Ereba: cassava bread

Ereba is a large, thin, crisp flatbread made from grated cassava that has been pressed in a long woven tube (a ruguma) to squeeze out the juice, then sifted and baked on a hot griddle. Making it is a communal task, and it keeps for weeks.

Other dishes

  • Darasa: grated green banana wrapped in leaves and boiled, a Garifuna tamale.
  • Bundiga: green banana and coconut, in the same family as hudut.
  • Tapou: a fish and green-plantain soup in coconut milk.

Where to try it

Hopkins and Dangriga are the places. Our sister site lists named Garifuna kitchens in Hopkins, such as Innie’s and Tugusina. If you are there around November 19, expect food stalls and celebrations.

Questions

Where is the best place to eat Garifuna food in Belize?

Hopkins and Dangriga on the southern coast. Order hudut ahead where possible.

When is Garifuna Settlement Day?

November 19 each year, a public holiday celebrated most strongly in Dangriga and Hopkins.
