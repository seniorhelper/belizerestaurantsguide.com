Know before you go

Eating in Belize, made easy: practical tips

Small things that make every meal smoother, from cash to closing days.

Money

Belize dollars and US dollars both work everywhere at a fixed 2:1 rate, so a BZ$20 plate is US$10. Change usually comes in Belize dollars. Cards are common at hotels and larger restaurants; small places may be cash only or add a fee. Our sister site has a tip and currency calculator.

Embrace "go slow"

Service is warm and unhurried, especially on the cayes. You may order at a counter, and no one will rush you to leave. Ask for the bill when you are ready.

Meet the hot sauce

Habanero sauce hits the table before the cutlery. Try a drop first.

Follow the locals

The best home-style kitchens rarely advertise. Ask where people go for ceviche, pigtail or hudut; locals love to point the way.

Mind closing days

Small family kitchens often close one day a week, and hours shift in the quieter low season. A quick check saves a wasted walk.

Seasons matter

Lobster is only legal July to February and conch from October until its quota closes it. See the calendar.

Meal times

Belizeans eat breakfast early, often by 7 a.m., and lunch is the main meal for many families, around noon. Dinner in restaurants usually starts around 6 p.m.; in small towns kitchens may close by 9 p.m. On Sundays many local places close, so plan ahead or look for hotel restaurants and Chinese restaurants, which tend to stay open.

Diet notes

Ask whether beans and rice were cooked with lard or meat; ask about wheat in fry jacks and johnny cakes; corn-based snacks are usually gluten-free. Our meal builder filters typical vegetarian and gluten-free dishes.

Practical questions

Do restaurants in Belize take credit cards?

Many do, but small island and roadside places may be cash only, and some add a card fee. Carry Belize or US dollars; the rate is fixed at 2 to 1.

How much should I tip in Belize?

About 10 to 15% at sit-down restaurants when no service charge is on the bill. At counters, rounding up is appreciated.

Can I see restaurant menus online before I go?

Increasingly, yes. Many Belize restaurants post menus on social media, and some use tap-to-open digital menus through services such as Link Restaurants, so a QR scan or NFC tap opens the current menu on your phone.
