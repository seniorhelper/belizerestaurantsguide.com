Food culture

Mestizo food: corn, masa and the north

Born of Maya and Spanish heritage and close ties to Mexico’s Yucatán, Mestizo cooking is Belize’s snack-and-soup tradition.

Mestizo Belizeans, many descended from refugees who crossed from the Yucatán during the 19th-century Caste War, are the country’s largest group today. Their food is strongest in Corozal, Orange Walk and Cayo, and on Ambergris Caye, where San Pedro was founded by Mestizo families. It leans on corn masa, citrus, achiote and pickled onions.

The masa snacks

Three snacks are everywhere and easy to confuse:

  • Salbutes: small corn tortillas that puff when fried, topped with shredded chicken, cabbage, tomato and sometimes avocado.
  • Garnaches: flat, crisp tortillas spread with refried beans and topped with cheese and pickled onion or cabbage.
  • Panades: half-moon corn turnovers filled with fish or beans, fried and served with onion relish.

Sold three or four for a few dollars, they make a quick lunch or a late-night snack.

Soups and stews

Escabeche in Belize is a sharp onion soup with chicken, vinegar, garlic and black pepper, served with corn tortillas; not the pickled fish of other countries. Relleno, sometimes called black dinner, is a dark soup made with black recado and often stuffed pork. Chirmole (chimole) is a similar black-recado chicken soup served at gatherings.

Recado

Recado is the seasoning paste behind much of this food. Red recado gets its color from annatto (achiote) and goes into stew chicken, escabeche and cochinita pibil, the slow-roasted pork wrapped in banana leaves. Black recado, made from charred chiles, makes relleno. Blocks of it are sold in markets.

Ceviche

Ceviche is a Mestizo favorite: shrimp, fish or conch cured in lime with onion, tomato, cilantro and habanero, served cold with tortilla chips. Conch is only legal in season; see the calendar.

Questions

What is the difference between salbutes and garnaches?

Salbutes are soft, puffed tortillas topped with chicken and cabbage; garnaches are crisp, flat tortillas with beans, cheese and pickled onion.

Is escabeche in Belize pickled fish?

No. In Belize it is a tangy onion-and-vinegar soup with chicken, served with corn tortillas.
