Beyond the grill

Cool off and branch out: ice cream, smoothies and international food in Belize

Belize’s food scene is not all seafood and stew. There is ice cream, smoothies and a growing international table.

Ice cream and gelato

Nothing beats a scoop after a hot afternoon. Look for tropical flavors such as coconut, soursop, mango and craboo (nance). Placencia’s Italian-style gelato is well known; see our sister site’s Placencia page.

Smoothies and chamoyadas

Fresh fruit smoothies (mango, pineapple, banana, soursop) are blended cold everywhere, and Mexican-style chamoyadas, fruit slush with sweet-sour chamoy and chili, have caught on in San Pedro.

The international table

Belize’s Chinese restaurants are a fixture of every town, a legacy of Chinese families who have lived here for generations; fried chicken and fried rice are cheap staples. East Indian families brought curries, and in the tourist towns you will find Italian, Thai, Mexican and American kitchens. On Ambergris Caye the variety is wide enough for a Thai-food night.

Chinese-Belizean food

Chinese restaurants in Belize serve a local style: fried chicken with chips, fried rice and chow mein, often in generous portions at low prices and open late. They are a reliable, inexpensive meal almost anywhere in the country, and a useful option on Sundays when other kitchens close.

Where to find them

San Pedro and Placencia have the most choice; San Ignacio has a lively mix too. See named places on Best Restaurants Belize.
