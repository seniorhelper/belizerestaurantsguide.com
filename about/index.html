About Belize Restaurants Guide

Who we are, how the site is funded, and how we check facts.

Belize Restaurants Guide is about the food of Belize itself: the dishes, the cultures that created them, the seafood seasons and the festivals. It is written for travelers who want to understand what is on the plate, not just find a table.

It is published by Zach Wennstedt of Eye To Ad Media, based in Denver, Colorado. We are independent of restaurants and tour companies.

Local businesses sometimes buy advertising here. Their ads appear in a box marked Sponsored, separate from our articles. For a town-by-town list of named restaurants, use our sister site Best Restaurants Belize.
