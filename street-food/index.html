Street food

Belizean street food: what to grab and how to order

Cheap, fast and delicious: the carts, stalls and shop counters where Belizeans eat on the go.

The classics

  • Salbutes, garnaches and panades: the Mestizo masa trio, three or four to a plate. See Mestizo food.
  • Tacos: small corn tortillas with shredded chicken or pork and onion relish, sold in the mornings from carts and doorways.
  • Tamales: wrapped in leaves and sold from coolers, often on weekends.
  • Meat pies: flaky pastry with minced beef, a Creole bakery staple.
  • Fry jacks and johnny cakes: breakfast from counters and carts.
  • BBQ chicken: grilled on oil-drum barbecues by the road, especially on weekends, with rice and beans or tortillas.
  • Coconut and fruit: fresh coconuts opened with a machete, cut mango with lime, and cups of fruit.

How to order

Most stalls sell by the piece or by the plate. Say how many you want ("four salbutes, please"), pay with small Belize dollar notes or coins, and add habanero sauce from the bottle at your own risk. Many carts sell out by early afternoon, and some only appear in the morning or evening.

Staying comfortable

  • Choose busy stalls with food cooked in front of you.
  • Hot food is the safest bet; skip anything that has sat out.
  • Carry small notes; change for large bills can be hard to find.

Where to look

San Pedro’s back streets and central park area, the San Ignacio Saturday market, Belize City’s markets, and roadside stands along the highways. San Pedro also has a container food park with several vendors; see our sister site’s San Pedro page.

Street dishes to know

Fry jacks

Puffed pillows of fried dough, filled or served with beans, eggs and cheese.

Meat pies

Small flaky pies of seasoned minced meat sold at shops and carts.

Salbutes

Puffed fried corn tortillas topped with shredded chicken, cabbage and tomato.

Garnaches

Crisp corn tortillas with refried beans, cheese and pickled onion.

Panades

Fried corn turnovers filled with fish or beans, with onion relish.

Tamales and bollos

Corn dough with seasoned meat, wrapped in banana leaf and steamed.

Ceviche

Shrimp, fish or conch cured in lime with onion, tomato, cilantro and habanero.

Coconut tart

Sweet pastry filled with grated coconut, sold at bakeries.
