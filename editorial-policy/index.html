Editorial policy and change log

Our standards, and what we corrected or removed.

The standards behind our articles.

Research

Facts about dishes, festivals and seasons come from government notices (such as the Belize Fisheries Department), established publishers, local news outlets and cultural sources, linked at the foot of each article.

Writing

We describe dishes in our own words, note where practice varies, and avoid claims such as "the best" unless a named source says so.

Advertising separation

Sponsored content sits in labeled boxes. Advertisers do not review or approve articles, and ratings shown inside an advertiser's artwork are the advertiser's own claims, not ours.

Images

Stock food photography is used to illustrate a type of food and is never labeled as a particular restaurant's dish.

Corrections

We fix errors quickly and log significant changes below.

Change log

  • Rating and review markup for Rainbow Grill & Bar: Removed the self-written Review and AggregateRating (5 out of 1) structured data and the "5-star" badges. The Rainbow Grill section is now labeled as a sponsored feature.
  • Lobster season dates: Corrected from "June 15 to February 14" to July 1 to the end of February (closed March 1 to June 30), per the Fisheries Department.
  • Restaurant picks by town: Moved to our sister site Best Restaurants Belize, which verifies and sources each listing. This site now focuses on dishes and food culture.
  • Advertiser flyers: Compressed (from about 22 MB to well under 1 MB on the page) and grouped in a labeled Sponsored strip.
