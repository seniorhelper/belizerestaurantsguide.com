A visitor market that keeps growing

Belize recorded 562,405 overnight visitors in 2024 in the Belize Tourism Board’s preliminary count, up 21% on 2023 and almost 12% above 2019. Cruise ships added another 764,993 passengers between January and November 2024.

Overnight visitors eat out two or three times a day for a week or more. Most have never heard of your restaurant before they land, so the guide they open on their phone shapes where they go.

Questions owners ask

Is a basic listing really free?

Yes. Restaurants in Belize can request an accurate basic listing on Belize Restaurants Guide for free: name, area, cuisine, price range, hours and links. We verify you’re the owner or manager before changes go live. Featured and Menu Pro are optional upgrades, and nothing you buy changes whether we keep you listed.

What is the difference between Featured and Menu Pro?

Featured ($29 a month) gives you a top spot on Belize Restaurants Guide with a badge, a photo, a pitch and your link. Menu Pro ($49 a month or $490 a year) includes the same Featured placement plus a hosted, Google-readable menu page, a full menu update every quarter and NFC table tags, all done for you.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No, and we won’t pretend it does. We promise the work: a clearly labeled top placement, your photo and link, and with Menu Pro a menu that search engines can read and that we keep current. How many diners choose you still depends on your food, reviews and service.

How fast does it go live?

Usually within 48 hours of getting your photo, pitch and link. Menu Pro menu pages go live within 48 hours of receiving your menu. We confirm you run the restaurant first, so a quick reply to our welcome email keeps things moving.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime online and stay live until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Menu Pro yearly is one payment of $490 for twelve months, which is two months free compared with paying monthly.

Do you list restaurants outside San Pedro?

Yes. The guide covers Ambergris Caye, Caye Caulker, Placencia, Hopkins, San Ignacio, Belize City and other towns visitors pass through. Featured spots go in the section for your town, so a Placencia restaurant is shown to people planning meals in Placencia, not in San Pedro.
